The Confident Wrong Answer →

One question, two search engines, 6,272 chunks of real session history. Keyword's top hit scored 0.833 and was completely wrong — a mobile build log. The correct answer scored 0.548 and contained none of the query's words. Why absolute similarity is not a quality signal, why a reasonable stopword rule deleted the only word that mattered, and the number to trust instead: signal = top − median.
